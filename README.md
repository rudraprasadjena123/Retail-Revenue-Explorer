# 📊 Retail Revenue Explorer

An Excel-based data analytics project that explores retail sales performance, product trends, customer ordering patterns, and delivery efficiency using sales data.

## 📌 Project Overview

Retail Revenue Explorer transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on analyzing revenue, identifying top-performing products, comparing sales across occasions and cities, and understanding delivery performance.

The goal is to support data-driven business decisions through data cleaning, analysis, and dashboard visualization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Calculate total sales revenue and average customer spending.
- Analyze monthly sales trends for 2023.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Explore order distribution across cities.
- Compare revenue across different gifting occasions.
- Analyze delivery duration and order quantities.
- Present insights through charts and dashboard visualizations.

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Load the order, product, and customer datasets.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Check missing values, data types, and duplicate records.
3. **Data Transformation:** Prepare dates, quantities, prices, and delivery-duration fields.
4. **Data Analysis:** Calculate sales metrics and summarize results by product, month, city, and occasion.
5. **Dashboard Creation:** Use Excel charts and PivotTables to visualize key findings.
6. **Business Insights:** Interpret patterns to identify potential sales and operational improvements.

## 💡 Business Value

This project demonstrates how retail data can help businesses:

- Understand sales trends and seasonal demand.
- Identify products that contribute strongly to revenue.
- Explore customer ordering patterns across locations.
- Compare product performance across gifting occasions.
- Investigate delivery efficiency and operational trends.

## ⚠️ Limitations and Data Privacy

- Results depend on the quality and definitions of the supplied dataset.
- Estimated revenue calculations must be validated against the meaning of the price field.
- Average order value should be calculated using unique orders, not simply the average transaction row.
- Correlation between quantity and delivery duration does not prove causation.
- Customer data should be anonymized before public sharing. Do not publish names, phone numbers, email addresses, or full addresses.
